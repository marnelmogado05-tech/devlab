# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DevLab is a place to mess around, get things wrong, and learn something. That only works if
getting things wrong is safe.

**Expected:**

- Assume good faith and a range of experience levels. Everyone was a beginner at the thing you
  find obvious.
- Critique the code, the challenge or the idea — not the person.
- Be direct and be kind. These are compatible.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no, and that a contributor may disagree.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including in challenge content
- Making someone feel stupid for a wrong answer or a basic question — DevLab is built on wrong
  answers
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review
- Sexualised content, or unwelcome attention of any kind

Developer humour is part of DevLab's character. Humour that punches down is not.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, submitted challenge content,
and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers via a private message or a GitHub security advisory
if no other private channel exists yet. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a
contributor, in proportion to the behaviour. Maintainers who do not act in good faith may be
removed from the project.
